Connect your account

Paste your agent token to get started. The app uses it to open tunnels for your local ports.

I don't have a token yet — how do I get one?
  1. Open the osmRouter dashboard and sign in (or create a free account).
  2. Go to Tokens in the sidebar.
  3. Click Create token, name it (e.g. “desktop”), and copy it — it looks like osm_xxxx.
  4. Paste it above and click Connect.
